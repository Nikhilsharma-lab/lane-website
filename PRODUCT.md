# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PMs and designers on a product design team. PMs arrive with problems (often disguised as solutions); designers own the craft of solving them and taking work from intake through to shipped. Design leads watch team *health*, never individual activity. Context of use: triaging incoming requests and moving design work through its stages — at a desk, focused, deciding what's worth building.

## Product Purpose

Lane is a design-ops platform built on one idea: **surveillance produces performance, support produces truth.** Requests come in, an AI intake gate turns solutions back into real problems, and designers take them through scientific stages to shipped — with no time tracking, no dashboards, no individual surveillance. Success looks like teams solving the *right* problems, with leads seeing health without watching people.

## Brand Personality

Premium and craft-forward — taste-led, precise, understated; the craft itself is the signal. Principled and confident (anti-surveillance), calm rather than loud. Voice is direct, opinionated, "built by a head of design." Three words: **premium, principled, crafted.**

## Anti-references

_Inferred from the built design + the brand-personality answer — refine freely._

- **Generic AI-SaaS:** no cream/sand body bg, gradient blobs, big-number hero-metric template, or endless identical icon-card grids.
- **Surveillance / productivity-tracking software:** the thing Lane opposes — charts everywhere, manager-watching-you dashboards.
- **Loud startup:** bright gradients, big emoji, bouncy/elastic motion, hype copy.
- Nothing that reads as cheap, templated, or spun-up-in-an-afternoon.

## Design Principles

- **Practice what you preach** — the product's restraint (problem-first, anti-surveillance) shows in a design that respects the reader and doesn't shout.
- **Show the real surface** — a true product window (list + detail with real IDs, avatars, stages, sign-offs), not an illustration.
- **Editorial over decorative** — serif display + mono labels carry the voice; texture (the halftone) grounds the composition, never competes with type.
- **Readability is non-negotiable** — copy never fights its background; if it's close, the background yields.
- **Craft as proof** — for a design-ops tool, taste and precision *are* the argument.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large/bold text ≥ 3:1. Visible keyboard focus (a `:focus-visible` ring on every interactive element). Reduced-motion alternatives for all reveals and animations (`prefers-reduced-motion`). Semantic structure and meaningful labels.
